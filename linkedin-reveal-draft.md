Most small shops don't have a sales problem.

They have an unanswered-message problem.

A customer messages at 9pm: "Is the large pepperoni available? How much
is delivery?" Nobody replies until morning. By then, they've ordered
somewhere else.

So I built a WhatsApp shop assistant that never leaves a message
unanswered.

It answers from the shop's own menu — prices, sizes, delivery times.
It takes the order, confirms it with the customer, and puts it straight
into the shop's orders list, with the total worked out to the cent. It
even knows what's sold out, and suggests something else instead.

And when the customer asks "where is my order?", it checks the real
orders and tells them. Even after closing time — the shop is shut, the
assistant still takes pre-orders for tomorrow.

This demo runs on a fictional pizza shop, SliceHouse Pizza — but the
assistant is real. Built with Groq Whisper, SenseNova, and Python.

The Agent Reveal series continues: agents that do the work.

If your business loses orders to unanswered messages, DM me. I'll show
you what this looks like on your business.
